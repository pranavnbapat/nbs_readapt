About this repository

This knowledge repository is part of ESPON ReAdapt — an applied research project supporting climate adaptation planning across ESPON territories. It brings together curated records on Nature-based Solutions (NbS) covering scientific papers, case studies, EU projects and policies from across Europe. Use it to explore evidence, find comparable cases, build an investment case, and navigate EU funding and policy alignment for NbS implementation.

ESPON ReAdapt

Europe's climate adaptation community has a knowledge problem — not a shortage of evidence, but a fragmentation of it. Peer-reviewed research, EU-funded project reports and national policy documents rarely reach the practitioners and planners who need them most.

The ReAdapt NbS Knowledge Repository addresses this by providing a single, searchable access point to curated evidence on Nature-based Solutions from across Europe — structured to support decision-making across research, planning and implementation.
